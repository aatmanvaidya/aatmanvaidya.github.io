Aatman Vaidya

aatmanvaidya [at] gmail.com

I am a Master’s student in Computational Linguistics at the University of Tübingen. I am a Research Assistant with Abhisek Dash at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI-SWS), and a part of the AI, Computing and Society initiative.

I am also advised by Kiran Garimella from Rutgers University, with whom I work on online platform-mediated harms and human-AI interaction. In Summer 2026, I was a Research Assistant at with Juhi Kulshrestha in the Computational Social Science group at Aalto University.

I work at the intersection of language, society and technology. My research interests broadly lie in computational social science and human centered AI, with a focus in measuring and mitigating online harms in low-resource contexts and making digital spaces safer and more inclusive.

Here are some directions that I work on and am excited about:

Platform accountability: I am interested in understanding how online platforms shape who is exposed to harm, and how can we reduce that exposure?
